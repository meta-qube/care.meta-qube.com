FAQ

Do you only work with AI-generated code?

No. We assess the system, its behaviour, and the development process, regardless of how the code was written.

Will we have to rewrite everything?

A rewrite is not the starting assumption. We compare local fixes, component changes, and broader modernisation against your goals and constraints.

Do we need to share source code now?

No. The first request only needs contact details and a description of the task. Access, confidentiality, and data processing are agreed before technical work.

Can we order an assessment without implementation?

Yes. An assessment is a separate engagement. Your team or another contractor can use its findings.

Can you support the system long term?

We can discuss responsibility for an agreed scope. Available expertise, access, working hours, response targets, and handover conditions are defined before an engagement starts.

How much does it cost?

The price depends on the task, system scope, available evidence, and responsibility required. We agree the scope and cost before paid work begins.

What is holding your software back?

Describe your situation and the result you need. We will review the request and contact you to clarify whether and how we can help.
